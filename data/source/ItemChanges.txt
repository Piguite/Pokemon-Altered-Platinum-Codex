=========================================================
Vitamin Replacements
=========================================================
Since Effort Values(EVs) have been removed, this means that vitamins have been removed, as well.
Each vitamin has been replaced with a corresponding consumable item.
Because hidden items are handled differently than visible ones, and no one has made a script editor capable of editing the global scripts that decide which
item you get from them, the hidden vitamins have instead been removed(placed so far off the map that they're unobtainable).
- HP Up >> Focus Sash
- Protein >> Enigma Berry
- Iron >> Sitrus Berry
- Calcium >> Power Herb
- Zinc >> Chesto Berry
- Carbos >> White Herb
PP Up and PP Max are unchanged, as they have nothing to do with Effort Values.
(Power items and Vitamins are still obtainable in the Battle Frontier, as these cannot be edited. The Battle Frontier itself is currently impossible to edit with my knowledge.)
