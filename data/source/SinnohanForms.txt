=========================================================
General Notes
=========================================================
- Sinnohan Forms are new regional variants with the same general concept as Alolan Forms- new type, abilities, stats and moves.
Unlike Alolan Forms, though, they totally replace the originals, due to engine limitations.
They also cannot breed.

There are a total of 30 Sinnohan Forms- three times the number of Alolan Forms, and 10 per region prior to Sinnoh.
None of these have been rushed, and were completed gradually over a long period of time as new ideas came in.
Though some might be a little overpowered, they were designed to be mostly balanced(compared to the new standard of power).
It's singleplayer anyway, so it's not like it matters too much!
A large blank space has been added below in case you decide to avoid spoilers.
